One image summing up Friday 20 November 2026's world news — six stories,
each verified to have happened or been first reported that day:

1. **Kyiv under fire** — Russia's biggest air raid of November: 400+ drones
   and 110 missiles hit Kyiv's bridges; at least 8 killed, missiles fall in Poland.
2. **Cyclone Akvan hits Odisha** — landfall near Gopalpur; 372,000 evacuated.
3. **COP31 runs into overtime** — Antalya talks miss their Friday deadline;
   the draft deal drops every mention of fossil fuels.
4. **EU and UK strike new deals** — Brussels summit: farm trade, shared carbon
   markets, youth travel for 100,000.
5. **Tanker hit in the Strait of Hormuz** — struck by a projectile off Oman,
   catches fire.
6. **Angola buys into De Beers** — $180m for 10% of the diamond giant.

The poster is drawn as HTML/SVG and rendered to `entry.png` (1024×1024,
under 1 MiB). `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies it to
`/output/entry.png`.

Run it: `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`
